## Numbers to answer with, and where they came from

- **Class distribution / 6 August question:** The *later verified* PlantVillage count is **38 classes, 54,305 images**. Largest: Orange Huanglongbing **5,507**; smallest: Potato healthy **152**; imbalance **36.2:1**. The saved 19 September run uses seed 42 and an **85/15** split: **46,160 training** and **8,145 validation**. It does not show a separate PlantVillage test split. Open [original notebook](../notebooks/originals/module-1.ipynb) cells 12 and 14 and the [October per-class CSV](evidence/module-1-candidate/class_distribution.csv). In the October adaptation, PlantDoc has **2,128 training**, **532 validation**, and **252 test** mapped images; this is a separate dataset/protocol. These counts are current evidence, not proof of work on 6 August.
- **Disease metrics:** The adapted CNN got **141/252 = 55.95%** PlantDoc test accuracy, macro F1 **0.552**; original checkpoint **78/252 = 30.95%**, macro F1 **0.297**, on the same test. The adapted model's PlantVillage validation accuracy is **96.83%** versus the original's **98.10%** on 8,145 images. Show the [results table](evidence/module-1-candidate/results_summary.csv), [per-class report](evidence/module-1-candidate/adapted_checkpoint__field_test_classification_report.csv), and [confusion counts](evidence/module-1-candidate/adapted_checkpoint__field_test_confusion_counts.csv). PlantDoc test was inspected in earlier work, so this is not a newly blind external test.
- **Canopy stress metrics:** Historical deployed RF: **66.75%** on a **400-tile random test** from a 1,996-tile sample, with weak healthy recall **7/67**. New **retrained**, grouped-field baseline: **58.42%**, macro F1 **0.474**, on **1,549 test tiles** after 5,175/1,584/1,549 train/validation/test split; healthy recall **23/280**, severe called healthy **74/700**. Show the [grouped split manifest](evidence/module-2-candidate/split_manifest.csv), [baseline report](evidence/module-2-candidate/rf_baseline__test_classification_report.csv), [confusion PNG](evidence/module-2-candidate/rf_baseline__test_confusion.png), and [decision](evidence/module-2-candidate/decision.json). ## Three locked base papers: the precise relationship

| Supplied PDF | What it studies | What CropSense actually uses |
|---|---|---|
| `mainPaper.pdf`: Shrotriya et al., IEEE Access 2024, DOI [10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3411013](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3411013) | Leaf-disease classification using clustering and neural networks, including an estimate of diseased area; its abstract reports 96–97% for selected diseases. | MobileNetV2 classification of 38 PlantVillage categories plus a separately mapped PlantDoc field-photo test. No diseased-area estimator and no directly comparable paper benchmark. |
| `supporting_1.pdf`: Le, Liou and Pham, IEEE Access 2023, DOI [10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3283033](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3283033) | Normalized Difference **Latent Heat** Index, surface-energy/evapotranspiration analysis of paddy fields and water stress. | Paired RGB/NIR **NDVI**, six pixel-summary features and a Random Forest. CropSense does not implement NDLI, thermal retrieval or evapotranspiration estimation. |
| `supporting 2.pdf`: Dong et al., IEEE JSTARS 2020, DOI [10.1109/JSTARS.2020.3013340](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSTARS.2020.3013340) | National-scale monitoring and forecasting with satellite, weather and biological inputs; wheat rust and locust examples. | Four-week weather/scouting LSTM on cotton Jassid/Aphid observations from Akola, compared with simpler baselines. The paper motivates the topic; its reported R² is not comparable with our three-class accuracy. |

The supplied PDFs establish related work, not that their models or reported scores were reproduced. The signed weekly sheets and any original raw files should be retained alongside this addendum rather than silently edited.
